# Lee Loves Padel

Two-on-two arcade padel that runs in the browser. Play as Lee with an AI partner, use the glass walls, and win the three-round World Tour.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. There's no build step and nothing to install.

To play it online, turn on GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → deploy from the `main` branch, root folder).

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Arrows / WASD | Move Lee, and aim at contact |
| J or Space | Drive, volley, or smash a high ball |
| K or Shift | Lob, or a bandeja on a high ball |
| P / Esc | Pause |

Press early: Lee winds up and swings when the ball arrives. Phones and tablets get an on-screen stick and buttons.

## Modes

- **World Tour**: Club Night, Costa Open, then the Glass Cup Final.
- **Quick Match**: easy, medium or hard opponents.
- Matches run to 2, 4 or 6 games. Golden point and auto-run can be toggled on the title screen.
